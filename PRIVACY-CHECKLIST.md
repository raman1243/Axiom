# Pre-launch privacy checklist

- Publish a privacy policy and a contact channel for corrections/removal requests.
- Obtain permission before displaying a phone number or personal address.
- Explain what information is public, why it is collected, and how long it is retained.
- Keep pending submissions private; do not expose rejected records through public APIs.
- Add a report/correction process and a documented response target.
- Restrict access to moderation notes and logs.
- Add bot protection and persistent rate limiting before promoting public submissions.
- Test database backup and restore, including who can access backups.
- Review applicable Indian privacy and consumer-protection requirements with a qualified professional before commercial launch.
